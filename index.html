1

Ask Instagram for your follower lists

Instagram lets anyone download a copy of their own data. You only need the "Followers and following" part. It's free and takes a few minutes.

  1. Open Instagram (the app or instagram.com) and go to your profile.
  2. Open the menu and go to:
    Settings›Accounts Center›Your information and permissions›Download your information
  3. Tap Download or transfer information, pick your account, then choose Some of your information.
  4. Tick only Followers and following and tap Next. Choose Download to device.
  5. Set these three options carefully:
    Date rangeAll time
    FormatJSON (not HTML)
    Media qualityAny (not used)
  6. Tap Create files. Instagram emails you when it's ready, usually within minutes but sometimes after a few hours. Download the ZIP file to your computer.
Choose "All time", not "Last year". With a shorter range, people who followed you before then would be missing, and the tool would wrongly say they don't follow you back.
Can't find it?

Instagram moves this menu sometimes. Try these:

  • In the Instagram settings search bar, type "Download your information".
  • Or go straight to accountscenter.instagram.com → Your information and permissions.
  • If you picked Google Drive instead of your device, open the folder in Drive, right-click it and choose Download. Drive gives you a ZIP.

What if it says a file is missing?

The tool needs these files from the export (inside connections/followers_and_following):

  • following.json: people you follow
  • followers_1.json (plus _2, _3 and so on for large accounts): people who follow you
  • pending_follow_requests.json: requests you sent that are still waiting (only included if you have any)

You can add files one by one. Each file adds to what's already loaded. If you only see HTML files, the export was made in HTML format. Request it again and choose JSON.

What do "Keep" and "Done" mean?
  • Keep protects an account, such as a celebrity, brand or friend you want to keep following even though they don't follow you back. Kept accounts are never included in a cleanup run.
  • Done means the account has been handled. The tool hides it so you can pick up where you left off next time.
  • Both are saved in this browser on this computer, along with your loaded lists, so you can close the page and come back tomorrow.

The script (read it before you run it, if you like)

This is exactly what gets copied. It only opens the profiles listed at the bottom and clicks Unfollow, and it doesn't send anything anywhere. Drag the bottom-right corner to make the box taller.

Then, on a computer (not your phone):
  1. Open instagram.com in Chrome or Edge and make sure you're logged in.
  2. Open the Console by pressing Ctrl + Shift + J⌘ Cmd + ⌥ Option + J . A panel opens at the side or bottom of the page. Don't worry: you can't break anything by looking.
  3. Click in the Console, paste with Ctrl + V and press Enter.
    If Chrome says "Don't paste code you don't understand", type allow pasting, press Enter, then paste again. That warning appears the first time for everyone.
  4. A small IG Cleanup box appears at the top right of Instagram. Click Start.
  5. A small window opens and works through the accounts. Leave both windows open. You can use other apps in the meantime. Click Stop at any time.
  6. When it finishes, the box shows the list of finished usernames, already selected. Copy it with Ctrl + C and paste it into step 5 below.
If Instagram says "Try Again Later", stop for the day. The script stops automatically when it sees that message. Tomorrow, pick the Careful speed. Blocks are temporary, but pushing through them makes them last longer.
The small window didn't open

Your browser blocked the pop-up. Look for the blocked pop-up icon at the right end of the address bar, choose Always allow pop-ups from instagram.com, then paste the script again.

It says it couldn't find the buttons

The script looks for the English words Requested, Following and Unfollow. If Instagram is set to another language, switch it to English (Settings›Language) while you run the cleanup, then switch back.

I'd rather do it by hand

That's the safest option. In step 3, click any username to open that profile, then tap Following or Requested → Unfollow. Afterwards, tick the account and click Mark as done.

5

Tick off what's finished

Paste the list from the Instagram box so the tool knows who's done. Those accounts disappear from your lists, and the next "Select the next 50" picks up where you left off.

Come back later for the next batch. Your lists and progress are saved in this browser. After a big cleanup, you can download a fresh export from Instagram to see your updated numbers.

Questions

Is this really private?

Yes. This page is a single file with no server behind it. The browser is told to block it from connecting to anything on the internet (a "Content Security Policy"), so it couldn't send your data anywhere, even by mistake. You can check the code yourself; it's all in one file.

The cleanup script runs inside your Instagram tab, using the login you already have. It never sees or asks for your password.

Can my account get banned?

Instagram limits how fast anyone can unfollow, and going too fast triggers a temporary "Try Again Later" block, usually for a few hours to a day. That's why the script waits a random time between accounts and stops as soon as Instagram complains.

Automating actions is against Instagram's Terms of Use, so there is always some risk. Keep runs small and use the Balanced or Careful speed. For zero risk, do it by hand from the lists in step 3.

Why not just use one of the "unfollow" apps?

Most of them ask for your Instagram password or full account access. That hands your account to a stranger's server. This tool never needs either.

Why do I need a computer? Can I use my phone?

You can do steps 1–3 on a phone, but step 4 uses the browser's Console, which only desktop browsers have. Chrome, Edge and Brave work best with the steps above. You can also do everything by hand on your phone using the lists.

How long will it take?

At the Balanced speed, about 100 accounts an hour. 1,000 old follow requests take roughly three or four days at 2–3 runs a day.

What is the "Console", and is it safe to paste things there?

It's a built-in developer tool in every desktop browser that lets you run small scripts on the page you're looking at. Chrome warns you the first time because you should only paste code you trust. This script is readable plain text; you can paste it into any AI assistant and ask what it does before running it.

The tool shows old numbers after I unfollowed people

The lists come from your export, which is a snapshot. Paste finished usernames into step 5 to hide them, or download a fresh export from Instagram and open it here again.
